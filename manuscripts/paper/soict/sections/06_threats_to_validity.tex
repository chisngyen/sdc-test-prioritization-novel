\section{Threats to Validity}
\label{sec:threats}

\enlargethispage{4.5\baselineskip}
\textbf{Construct Validity}: While APFD is standard, ties can distort rankings; we mitigate this via 30 randomized tie-breaking trials, reporting mean, SD, and ROC-AUC. \textbf{Internal Validity}: Preprocessing pipelines and random seeds ($42$) were fixed across baselines; normalization statistics and SWA checkpoints were computed strictly on training folds. \textbf{External Validity}: We evaluated generalizability across four simulators (BeamNG.tech, Gazebo/\allowbreak PX4, DeepDriving, AsFault) spanning 2D driving and 3D flight. Physical track testing remains future work.
