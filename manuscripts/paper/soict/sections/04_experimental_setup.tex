\section{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:setup}

To rigorously evaluate the geometric robustness, continuous dynamics, and prioritization efficacy of CliffordTrajNet, we conduct empirical evaluations across both 2D ground and 3D aerial autonomous benchmarks.

\subsection{Datasets and Benchmarks}

\textbf{SensoDat Benchmark}~\cite{sensodat}: The master autonomous driving suite comprising $32{,}580$ executed BeamNG.tech simulation tests generated by three search-based test generators (Frenetic, FreneticV, AmbieGen) across multiple autonomous driving agents. We adopt the standard partition of $28{,}804$ training tests and $3{,}776$ validation tests, evaluated on the official $956$-test competition benchmark suite~\cite{roadfury}.

\textbf{Cross-Benchmark SDC Suite}: To evaluate cross-domain transferability without per-task tuning, we assess nine external testbeds spanning over $43{,}000$ simulation tests: (i)~\emph{SDC-Scissor}~\cite{sdcscissor} ($N=201$, 5-fold CV); (ii)~\emph{ITS4SDC}~\cite{its4sdc} ($N=10{,}000$ sequence trajectories); (iii)~\emph{SDC-Travel}~\cite{sdcprio} ($N=14{,}166$ across 66 test-generator campaigns); (iv)~\emph{SensoDat-OOB}~\cite{sensodat} (mild departure $\tau=0.1$\,m and moderate departure $\tau=0.3$\,m); and (v)~\emph{Physical Dynamics Suites}~\cite{sdcprio} (BeamNG-RF at $1.0\times$, $1.5\times$, and $2.0\times$ friction, alongside DriverAI).

\textbf{UAV Testing Competition (Aerialist / PX4 3D)}: The official benchmark from the SBFT UAV Testing Competition~\cite{uavcomp}, comprising 3D waypoint mission trajectories executed on PX4 Autopilot in Gazebo with environmental wind perturbations and obstacle avoidance maneuvers. Critical failure is defined as obstacle clearance $< 8.0$m or geofence breach.

\subsection{Evaluation Metrics}

We evaluate prioritized test execution using standard software testing metrics: (1)~\textbf{Multi-Trial APFD}: Mean and standard deviation over $30$ randomized tie-breaking trials~\cite{roadfury}; (2)~\textbf{ROC-AUC}: Threshold-free discriminative capability; (3)~\textbf{Rotation Drift ($\Delta\mathrm{APFD}_{\mathrm{rot}}$)}: Maximum absolute drift across planar rotations $\{0^\circ, +30^\circ, +60^\circ, +90^\circ, +180^\circ, -45^\circ\}$ for 2D SDC and 3D rotations for UAVs; (4)~\textbf{Resolution Drift ($\Delta\mathrm{APFD}_{\mathrm{res}}$)}: Variation across five waypoint densities ($N \in \{64, 96, 128, 160, 197\}$); (5)~\textbf{Curvature Violation Rate ($\%V$)}: Percentage of test pairs where higher curvature $\kappa_{\max}$ is erroneously assigned lower risk; and (6)~\textbf{Conformal Safety Recall}: Empirical percentage of critical failures caught under PAC risk level $\epsilon$.

\subsection{Baseline Techniques}

We benchmark CliffordTrajNet against representative methods spanning five distinct groups: (1)~\textbf{Uninformed \& Search-based}: Random ordering, Single-Objective SDC-Prioritizer~\cite{sdcprio}, and Greedy Diversity~\cite{sdcprio}. (2)~\textbf{Feature-based ML}: SDC-Scissor~\cite{sdcscissor} (Random Forest and Gradient Boosting over 14 tabular features) and ITEP4SDC~\cite{roadfury} (3-feature MLP). (3)~\textbf{Alternative Deep Models}: 3-layer GCN~\cite{gcn}, ResNet-50 visual CNN~\cite{resnet}, and zero-shot LLMs. (4)~\textbf{Deep Sequence Baselines}: ITS4SDC~\cite{its4sdc} (Bi-LSTM) and our competition-winning baseline RoadFury~\cite{roadfury} (Pre-LN Transformer with SWA on 10 coordinate channels). (5)~\textbf{UAV Baselines}: Obstacle Proximity Heuristics, Surrealist~\cite{roadfury}, TUMB~\cite{tumb}, PRGenUAV-LFS~\cite{uavcomp}, and OptObstacles~\cite{optobstacles}.

\subsection{Implementation and Hardware}

CliffordTrajNet is implemented in PyTorch 2.4 using mixed-precision (AMP bf16). Training was executed on NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 and NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5070 GPUs with AdamW (learning rate $\eta = 5 \times 10^{-4}$, weight decay $10^{-4}$). Model training uses fixed random seed $42$ to support reproducibility; APFD evaluations use 30 randomized tie-breaking seeds.
